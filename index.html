      All figures come from a simulation, not from sensors. Assumed values: hourly use pattern, sensor noise of 0.05 L/min, a 0.1 L/min leak, repair the day after confirmation, and a valve that closes instantly. Flow rate of 7.6 L/min for a 2.0 gpm faucet (EPA WaterSense). Cooling: a 1 MW hybrid site with 500 kW of dry coolers (assumed), 1.9 L per kWh for evaporative cooling (EESI average), and a heat wave and workload burst added on purpose. Blocks B and C are simulated peers. The 50 L per person per day benchmark is the basic requirement used in a Nsukka household study (Nigerian Journal of Technology).
